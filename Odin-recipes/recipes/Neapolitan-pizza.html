Neapolitan Pizza


Description

Neapolitan pizza is a round, historic style from Naples, Italy,
featuring a thin, soft center and a high, airy, puffed crust with charred "leopard spots".
Baked in a wood-fired oven at very high heat for just 90 seconds

Ingredients

  • Flour: 500g Type "00" pizza flour (or bread flour if unavailable)
  • Water: 325ml warm water
  • Yeast: 2g active dry or instant yeast
  • Salt: 10g to 12g fine sea salt
  • Toppings: Canned crushed San Marzano tomatoes, fresh mozzarella (torn and drained), fresh basil leaves, and olive oil

Steps

  1. Mix the Dough: Dissolve the yeast in warm water.
    Pour it slowly into the flour and salt mixture.
    Stir with a spoon until it forms a shaggy mass,
    then knead with your hands for 10 minutes until smooth.
  2. First Rise: Cover the dough with a damp cloth and let it rest at room
    temperature for about 2 hours until it doubles in size.
  3. Divide and Shape: Punch the air out gently.
    Divide the dough into two or three equal portions (about 250g–290g each).
    Roll each piece into a tight, smooth ball.
    Cover and let rest for 1.5 more hours.
  4. Stretch the Base: Gently press the center of a dough ball
    outward with your fingertips to push air toward the edges,
    leaving the outer rim thick to form the crust.
    Stretch it out carefully by hand without rolling pin.
  5. Top and Bake: Spread a thin spoonful of tomato sauce in the center,
    add fresh basil, torn mozzarella, and a drizzle of olive oil.
    Bake on a preheated heavy baking sheet or pizza stone at your
    oven's maximum heat setting (around 500°F/260°C) for 5 to 7 minutes
    until the crust is puffed and lightly charred.
